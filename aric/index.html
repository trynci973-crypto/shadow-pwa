The entrance

📷 Entrance photo coming soon

The entrance is on Tennis Place itself, not on Dartmouth Street or around the corner. It is in the 6-story apartment building, the Tennis View co-op. The building's official address is 4 Dartmouth Street. Look for No. 6 on the Tennis Place side.

Map apps can drop you around the corner on Dartmouth St or at the wrong door. Use the walking button above, which goes straight to the Tennis Place door. If you're unsure, call 718-793-6072.

From Forest Hills–71 Av subway (E F M R)

  1. Take the exit on the south side of Queens Blvd at 71st Ave / Continental Ave.
  2. Walk south on 71st Ave (Continental) past Austin St. Go under the LIRR overpass into Station Square (about 4 min).
  3. From Station Square, walk west along Burns St to Tennis Place.
  4. Turn left onto Tennis Place. No. 6 is a short way down on the left, facing Tennis Place.

From LIRR Forest Hills station

  1. Leave the station down the stairs into Station Square.
  2. Walk west along Burns St to Tennis Place (about 2 min).
  3. Turn left onto Tennis Place. No. 6 is on the left, facing Tennis Place.
